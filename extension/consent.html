Before Midas hears a tab

Midas for Chrome works with the Midas app on this Mac. Please read what it does before you allow it.

What it sends

When you click the Midas button on a tab, that tab's audio goes to the Midas app on this Mac, which transcribes it. Only that tab, and only until you click the button again or close the tab. The button reads ON meanwhile, and the tab keeps playing for you.

Where it goes

The extension sends the audio only to the Midas app on this Mac, through Chrome's native messaging; it sends nothing over the internet. In the Midas app the transcript stays on your Mac, unless you ask its Claude copilot for an answer: then the recent text of the meeting goes to Anthropic to write it. With a local Ollama model, nothing leaves your Mac.

On Google Meet

The extension reads one thing on the page: whether your microphone is muted, so Midas mutes its own microphone with you. It reads nothing else.

The other people in the call

The transcript holds what they say too. Tell them you're transcribing, and get their consent where the law or your organization requires it.
