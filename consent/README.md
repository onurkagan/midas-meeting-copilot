# Consent and notice templates

Starting points for telling meeting participants about Midas and, where needed, asking for their consent. **These are templates, not legal advice.** Laws differ by country, state and sector; have a lawyer or your data protection officer review them before you rely on them.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [meeting-notice.md](meeting-notice.md) | A short message for the meeting chat, a sentence to say out loud, and a line for the calendar invite (English and Turkish). Midas's reminder card copies the chat message for you. |
| [privacy-notice.en.md](privacy-notice.en.md) | A privacy notice (GDPR Art. 13 style) when an organization uses Midas in its meetings. |
| [privacy-notice.tr.md](privacy-notice.tr.md) | Aydınlatma metni (KVKK m.10), for Turkey. |
| [consent-form.en.md](consent-form.en.md) | A separate consent form, where consent is the legal basis. |
| [consent-form.tr.md](consent-form.tr.md) | Açık rıza metni, for Turkey. |

## How to use them

1. **Choose the copilot first.** With the Ollama copilot and `local_only = true`, the meeting stays on your Mac. With Claude, the transcript text goes to Anthropic in the United States, which adds a recipient and an international transfer to every document.
2. **Fill in the brackets.** Replace each `[…]` and delete the options that don't apply.
3. **Keep the privacy notice and the consent form separate.** Many regimes expect consent to be a separate, specific and freely given choice. In Turkey, the KVKK Board's principle decision 2026/347 requires the aydınlatma metni and the açık rıza metni to be separate documents, never combined or accepted with a single click.
4. **Respect a "no".** If someone objects, go off the record (⌃⌥P) or stop Midas for that meeting.

## Before you rely on them

- **Consent to record:** many US states (California, Florida, Illinois, Washington and others) and many countries require everyone's consent. Midas doesn't record audio, but courts may still treat transcription as recording.
- **Turkey:** whether Turkish Penal Code article 133 (recording conversations) and the KVKK's personal-use exemption (m.28) cover your use hasn't been checked for these templates.
- **Workplaces:** works councils (for example in Germany) may need to approve tools like Midas.
- **Professional secrecy:** lawyers, doctors and therapists often have stricter duties. Use the Ollama copilot and `local_only`, and check your professional rules.
